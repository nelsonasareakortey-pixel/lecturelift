How lecture context works

When enabled, LectureLift sends the selected lecture's saved notes, questions and answers with your question. This helps the AI give lecture-specific explanations. If no lecture is selected, you can still ask general study questions.

Chat history is kept in this browser session only. Avoid entering private or sensitive information.
